# UI Goes Modular

Drone music has had a profound influence on my sound explorations. Substained tones, sine clusters, very minimal harmonic variations they're all entangled with the concept of time in the sonic realm. Drone acts like a suspension of the moment and it has the power of bringing you in undefined territories.
This is a small collection of lust drones I've made using the amazing Reaktor instrument DRON-E designed by Antonio Blanca.

Just drop me an email if you are interested and I will send you the download link.
